# Questions for Incubyte

These are proposed clarifications, not questions already sent or answers received.

1. I propose modelling salary as annual gross base salary, excluding bonuses, benefits, taxes and payroll payments. Is that the intended scope, or should compensation include additional components?
2. For employees in different countries, may reports keep each currency separate, or do you expect conversion to a common reporting currency? If conversion is expected, which exchange-rate source/date should be used?
3. Does “answer questions about how the org pays people” mean dashboards and filterable reports, or is a natural-language AI question interface expected in the product? I understand that using AI during development is mandatory in either case.
4. I propose CSV validation/import for new employees, salary history and employee deactivation, with no payroll execution or approval workflow. Is there a particular HR workflow you would like prioritized instead?
5. Is there a submission deadline or a preferred way to provide demo access and the video link?

While awaiting answers, the requirements document labels the proposed choices explicitly. A reviewer should not interpret them as confirmed business rules.
